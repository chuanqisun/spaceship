bias field — candidate biases as a living interaction graph

Each candidate is a node; faint edges are learned interactions between biases (tension feeds wry, hushed dampens brisk…). Nodes drift under repulsion and settle into a loose constellation. The hot attractor is the blend: pull it toward a node and that bias weighs more; edges to it thicken with its weight. Drag nodes to rearrange the field — the graph remembers the relationships even as you compose.
