bias bloom — pull a petal out for strength, bend it sideways for value

A night-sky flower as default, with the fan one click away: each candidate owns one petal at a fixed compass bearing. Drag along a petal to set its absolute strength 0–100% — like a knob's distance today, no sum-to-100 — and drag across it to swing its value. Click a petal and it blooms into the app's own fan: the same word arc magnified, the same needle as the pull-line, the same 25·50·75 rings, the same name: value % readout — the fan is a petal in close-up. Double-click or Esc closes it; touch another petal and the fan turns to face it.
