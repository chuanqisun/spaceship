text / style 03 — telemetry

Denser and more quantitative. White is the user; cyan is the machine: tokens arriving, a menu loading, a control being realized. Attention is drawn as a bar under each word whose height is its weight, so the spread around a keyword reads as a spectrum, not a blur. Influence prints its number. A status line reports what the model is doing right now.
