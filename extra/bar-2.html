text / left rail 2 — profile

The same ruler, but connection strength is drawn as one continuous profile down the lines: a smoothed curve through each line's strength, filled toward the text. It reads as a distribution, showing where in the story the focus draws from, more than as separate numbers. Keyword relevance sits on a dot plot: a 0–1 track on each keyword's line with a dot at its score, and the numeral beside it.

01live

Hover the main knob, or press focus tip, to see each line's connection to the sentence under it. Hover a shaded box to see connection to its phrase. re-measure simulates the knob moving: the decision API scores every keyword against the new tip.

02states

03encoding

ruler
as bar 1: spine against the text, tick per line, numbered every fifth, tip tick 1.5px.
rel lanes
numeral, then a 0–1 hairline track with a 5px dot at the score. Hovered box: dot and numeral orange. Measuring: dashed orange track, no dot.
profile
a smooth curve through one point per line (short ink-3 dash on each), area filled at .10 toward the text. Guides at .25 / .5 / .75 like the HUD's strength rings.
focus
spine thickens beside the focus sentence; focus-only lines print tip / box and the curve dips to 0 there.
live
the curve and fill turn orange while any point moves, and settle to white when the last one lands.

04data

connection
per sentence, from the influence source, against the focus (sentence under the knob, or the hovered box's phrase); each line averages the sentences it holds, weighted by characters.
relevance
per box keyword, from the decision API: how relevant the keyword is to the tip (the sentence under the main knob); re-measured when the knob moves.
refactor
one scorer for both focuses, returning raw strengths; the rail, not the text, applies contrast. Lines are the unit of display, sentences the unit of scoring.

05motion

0ms
the focus itself: hovering a box or the knob switches focus instantly; keyword and bracket turn orange at once.
300ms out-expo
each line's value moving to its new strength, and each score counting to its new value.
18ms per line
stagger outward from the focus sentence, so the update ripples from where the pointer is.
orange while rising
a line or score turns orange while it grows and settles to white when it lands. Falling and draining (leaving the focus) stay white: the hand stopped asking.
650ms (simulated)
decision API latency for keyword relevance; scores pulse orange until they arrive.
