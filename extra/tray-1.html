text / tray 1 — polybar modules

Borrowed from polybar and waybar: the model and text labels become one glyph each, values sit flush beside it, and hairline separators group the fields into three zones — what the model is doing on the left, what the text holds in the middle, what steers it on the right. Buffer folds into words as a +n suffix. Steering counts and sound keep short words. Nothing is removed; the strip drops from 31px to 18px and never wraps.
