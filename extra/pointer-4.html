text / pointer 4 — steering

The hand as a steering vector. Instead of dragging the main knob, the hand points somewhere ahead in the text and the knob is pulled there along the reading order: fast when the hand points far ahead, slowing as it arrives, never past it. A line from the knob to the hand shows the pull, orange while pulling because the knob is changing, with a tick per five words still to go; a caret in the text marks where the knob is heading. It works with either pointer: the vector is drawn under it.

01try

idle words0 boxes1

Point ahead of the main knob in the text and press anywhere (not on a knob or control) to pull it there; let go to stop. With hover pulls, pointing ahead is enough. Point behind it to see that nothing pulls.

02behavior

pointing ahead, engaged
the knob is pulled toward the word under the hand at 2.5× the gap per second (3 to 40 words/s); the vector is orange, ticked every 5 words to go, the destination caret orange; the readout: +12 words · 30 w/s.
arriving
the pull slows with the gap; the ticks drop off one by one; at the destination the vector ends.
pointing behind the knob
nothing pulls: the vector is dashed ink-4, no destination, the readout says so. Moving back stays a deliberate drag.
engaging
press to pull: hold anywhere that is not a knob or a control. hover pulls: whenever the hand is ahead of the knob in the scene; stronger, and easier to trigger by accident.
with either pointer
ring or crosshair keep their own behavior at the hand; the vector and destination are drawn beneath them.

03principles

never locks
the pointer is always exactly where the hand is; it never snaps to or reshapes around geometry.
elements wake by vicinity
within 120px a target reacts in proportion to nearness (eased): a knob's hover ring fades in, the attention box deepens, a tray control brightens. Full hover state only on touch.
one nearest
only the nearest target is pointed to (tick, tether, strongest frame, label); everything else stays quiet.
orange = live
only while the hand presses or drags, or the steering vector pulls; while dragging, nothing else wakes.
latency
a page-drawn pointer trails the hardware one by about a frame. It is drawn straight from the pointer event, never on a later frame, and only on desktop pointers; touch keeps none.
