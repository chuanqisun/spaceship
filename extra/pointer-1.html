text / pointer 1 — crosshair

Two hairlines edge to edge at the hand's exact position, as in CAD; they never snap. Idle they sit at ink-5. Guidance comes from two places: the elements themselves wake as the hand nears them (a knob's ring fades in, the box deepens, a tray control brightens), and where the nearest target's center crosses each hairline a short tick marks it, stronger the nearer it is, so the hand can see how far it is from lining up.

02behavior

idle
hairlines at ink-5 with a 12px gap around the point; nothing else.
approaching a target
within 120px, the target wakes in proportion to nearness, and alignment ticks appear on both hairlines where its center crosses them.
near the attention box
the box's shading deepens; at close range its relevance reads beside the point: box .91.
near the main knob
its hover ring fades in; close up the readout says how many words it shows.
pressing, dragging
hairlines go a soft orange and ticks orange while the hand changes something; nothing else wakes meanwhile.

03principles

never locks
the pointer is always exactly where the hand is; it never snaps to or reshapes around geometry.
elements wake by vicinity
within 120px a target reacts in proportion to nearness (eased): a knob's hover ring fades in, the attention box deepens, a tray control brightens. Full hover state only on touch.
one nearest
only the nearest target is pointed to (tick, tether, strongest frame, label); everything else stays quiet.
orange = live
only while the hand presses or drags, or the steering vector pulls; while dragging, nothing else wakes.
latency
a page-drawn pointer trails the hardware one by about a frame. It is drawn straight from the pointer event, never on a later frame, and only on desktop pointers; touch keeps none.
