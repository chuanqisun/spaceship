text / tray 3 — corner gauges

Borrowed from btop and conky desktops: replace the full-width footer with a 12px cluster in the bottom-right corner, so the page gets its whole bottom row back. Words are cut almost entirely. A braille sparkline of the rate replaces the state word, since orange and moving means streaming. A 32px gauge replaces the buffer count. Pips replace the small counts: □ per box, ◇ per steering knob, filled ◆ when pinned. First chunk moves into a tooltip. Hovering the cluster raises a short label above each part.
