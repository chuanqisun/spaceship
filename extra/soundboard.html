text / soundboard — signal × telemetry

Sound follows the same two inks as the picture. Your hand makes clicks: short filtered-noise transients, the friction of the instrument. The model makes tones: small FM blips with a slightly metallic ratio, the audible version of orange. When something settles, it ends on a plain sine. Every pitch comes from one pentatonic scale, so overlapping sounds never clash. ▶ auditions a variant; the dot selects it. Click the dot again to clear it. Every event is optional. Round 2: last round's picks are now option a and preselected. Generation is silent. Revealing words by hand plays a quiet tick. All deletions share one sound, and pin has new confirmation candidates.

01grammar

hand · ink
Bandpass-noise clicks, 1–4 kHz, 3–6 ms, sometimes with a sine body. Heard only while your hand moves something: grab, release, detents, steps. They are the haptics.
model · hot
FM blips, ratio 3.01. Heard when the model hands you something: a menu loading or arriving, or a realization score. Orange, made audible. Background generation is silent because it starts and stops too often to be worth hearing.
reveal · hush
Revealing and scrubbing words is the most frequent sound, so it is the quietest and plainest: one fixed-pitch tick under 10 ms, slightly higher forward and lower back. It plays at most once per 30 ms and softens as you drag faster. Lines and the column edge have no sound of their own.
settled · sine
A plain sine closes a state. The margin pin is the strongest case and the only resolved chord on the board: a live control becoming a stable fact.
removal · dash out
One sound for every deletion, whether box or future knob. It mirrors the spawn's two dashes, played outward and falling, so creation and removal are a recognizable pair.
provisional · breath
Dashed states (choosing, loading, a new knob) use airy noise. Committed states (tuned, taken, pinned) use tones, just as dashed lines turn solid.
pitch
E-major pentatonic, E4 to E7. Higher means forward, more, added. Lower means back, less, removed. Values such as score, strength, angle and word count map onto the scale, so the pitch tells you the value.
time
Same tokens as motion. Feedback stays under 120 ms and most of it under 30 ms. A measured value arriving takes 300 ms. Only the margin pin lasts 600 ms. Sounds under the pointer start within one frame and are never eased.
space
Stereo pan follows screen x. The pin sounds where it lands, in the margin.
loudness
One-shots on top, continuous textures about 20 dB lower, and the word tick quietest of all. Silence is the default state.
